   A. 10 km East  <-- ANSWER

   C. 10 km West
   D. 15 km East
   Why: North 10 km (facing N). Turn right -> facing East, walk 5 km. Turn right again -> facing South, walk 10 km (cancels the first North 10 km). Turn left from South -> facing East, walk 5 km more East. Net: vertical cancels to 0; horizontal = 5+5 = 10 km East.

12. [Direction Sense / Direction & Distance (Pythagorean) / lvl3] A man walks 8 km East, then turns and walks 6 km North. How far is he from his starting point, and in which general direction does that displacement lie?

   B. 14 km, North-East
   C. 10 km, East
   D. 10 km, North
   Why: The two legs (8 km East, 6 km North) are perpendicular, so the straight-line distance = sqrt(8^2+6^2) = sqrt(100) = 10 km. Since the net displacement has both an Eastward and a Northward component, its direction is North-East.

2. [Geometry / Direct Common Tangent / lvl3] The centres of two circles are 25 cm apart. Their radii are 9 cm and 2 cm. Find the length of their direct common tangent.
   A. 24 cm  <-- ANSWER
   B. 23 cm
   C. 20 cm
   D. 21 cm
   Why: Length of direct common tangent = sqrt(d^2 - (r1-r2)^2) = sqrt(25^2 - 7^2) = sqrt(625-49) = sqrt(576) = 24 cm.

3. [Mensuration / Sphere Melted into Spheres (count) / lvl3] A solid sphere of radius 10 cm is melted and recast into small solid spheres each of radius 2.5 cm. How many small spheres are formed?
   A. 64  <-- ANSWER
   B. 32
   C. 48
   D. 80
   Why: Volume is conserved. Number of small spheres = (Big radius / Small radius)^3 = (10/2.5)^3 = 4^3 = 64.

4. [Geometry / Parallel Chords (reverse for radius) / lvl3] Two parallel chords of lengths 16 cm and 12 cm lie on opposite sides of the centre of a circle, 14 cm apart. Find the radius of the circle.
   A. 10 cm  <-- ANSWER
   B. 9 cm
   C. 12 cm
   D. 11 cm
   Why: Let the chords' distances from the centre be d1 and d2, with d1+d2=14. From the chord-radius relation: r^2 = 8^2+d1^2 = 6^2+d2^2. Subtracting: d2^2-d1^2=28, and since d2+d1=14, (d2-d1)=28/14=2. So d1=6, d2=8. r^2 = 8^2+6^2 = 100, r = 10 cm.
