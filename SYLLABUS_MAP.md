## 1. Exam Pattern (verified)

| Stage | Paper | Content | Q / Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prelims** | Paper-I | Arithmetic & Reasoning/Mental Ability (SSC standard) | 100 / 100 | 180 min |
| | Paper-II | General Studies (Degree standard) | 100 / 100 | 180 min |
| **Mains** | Paper-I | English (Descriptive, Degree) — qualifying | — / 100 | 180 min |
| | Paper-II | Telugu or Urdu (Descriptive, Degree) — qualifying | — / 100 | 180 min |
| | Paper-III | Arithmetic + Reasoning (Objective) | 200 / 200 (PC-11) or 200 / 100 (PC-13) | 180 min |
| | Paper-IV | General Studies (Objective, Degree) | 200 / 200 (PC-11) or 200 / 100 (PC-13) | 180 min |

No negative marking (confirmed from Mains 2023 paper header). Qualifying marks: 40% OC, 35% BC, 30% SC/ST/ESM per paper.

**Verified GS subject distribution from Mains 2023 Paper-IV (200Q, Booklet A):**

| Subject | Questions observed | Share |
|---|---|---|
| General Science (Phy ~10, Chem ~10, Bio ~20) | ~40 | ~20% |
| Current Affairs (incl. AP, economy data, schemes, summits, sports, awards) | ~80 | ~40% |
| History + INM (Ancient/Medieval/Modern + Freedom) | ~28 | ~14% |
| Geography (India + physical + census data) | ~20 | ~10% |
| Polity (Constitution articles, amendments, Parliament, PIL) | ~20 | ~10% |
| Economy (rural, schemes, trade, budget) | ~12 | ~6% |

Key observation: **~40% of the 200Q Mains GS paper is Current Affairs** (national + international + AP). Science is Degree-level (pH calculations, coordination numbers, quantum numbers, plant physiology, anatomy) — not school-level. Polity questions are article-exact and statement-based.

## 4. 16-Week Plan

Assumptions: A (primary student) studies 5–6 hrs/day on portal + offline; B (inconsistent) follows a reduced subset. Prelims is 6 weeks away; Mains ~16 weeks. English + Telugu descriptive run parallel from Week 3 onwards (light load first, heavier after Prelims).

### Phase 1: Prelims Focus (Weeks 1–6)

**Week 1 — Arithmetic Core + Polity consolidation**
- Arithmetic: Number System (1.1), Percentage (1.4), Ratio & Proportion (1.2), Average (1.3). Target: 45Q practice across topics.
- Polity: Use existing portal. Day 1–3 bank (day01–03 = FR, Arts 14–22, DPSP). Complete all 3 days (135Q).
- Current Affairs: Start daily 15-min CA habit (national + AP). Log 5 facts/day in portal CA module.

## 5. Completion Standards (per topic)

A topic is "complete" when ALL of these are true for A:

| Level | Requirement | Tracking |
|---|---|---|
| **Notes read** | Topic page opened + "Mark as complete" ticked | `si_done` → `study_progress` |
| **Practice done** | ≥30Q attempted on the topic (topic bank + mixed) | `question_attempts` |
| **Accuracy** | ≥80% correct on topic-specific Qs | `topic_mastery.accuracy` |
| **Speed** | ≥20s average per Q in timed mode | `exam_attempts.time_taken` |
| **Trap awareness** | Top 3 `trapcat` misidentified, re-answered correctly on retry | `question_attempts` re-attempt linked |
| **Mock score** | Full mock section score ≥70% for that subject | `exam_attempts` |

Exception: **Current Affairs** — standard is ≥70% accuracy (volatile content). **English/Telugu Descriptive** — standard is 1 timed practice per format reviewed by mentor (no accuracy %).
