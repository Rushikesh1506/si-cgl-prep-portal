## 15. Berubari — Can India give away land? (1960)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: Preamble was only a key in 1960, not a part; cession of Indian territory needs Article 368 amendment, not Article 3 law. Year: 1960, 9th Amendment followed. Later Kesavananda (1973) said Preamble IS part, amended once only by 42nd (1976). SI relevance: cession question = amendment; Preamble date 26 Nov 1949 is adoption, not commencement.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Cession of Berubari to Pakistan needs only Article 3 law." — WRONG. Right logic: cession needs Article 368 amendment; Article 3 is for internal reorganisation. 9th Amendment 1960 proves it.
2. "Berubari held Preamble is part of Constitution." — WRONG. Right logic: Berubari 1960 said NOT part; Kesavananda 1973 said IS part. Examiner swaps years — remember 1960 No, 1973 Yes.
3. "Preamble was amended many times / date in Preamble is commencement (26 Jan 1950)." — Both WRONG. Right logic: amended only once (42nd, Socialist Secular Integrity); date in Preamble is adoption (26 Nov 1949).

## 16. Zee Telefilms v. BCCI — Is BCCI State? (2005)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: BCCI is NOT State under Article 12, so Article 32 fails; but it does public functions, so Article 226 writ in High Court lies. Year: 2005. SI relevance: private body doing public job (school, sports body, contractor) can still face High Court writ though Supreme Court Article 32 is closed.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Zee Telefilms held BCCI is State under Article 12." — WRONG. Right logic: held NOT State — no govt money, no govt control. Article 32 petition not maintainable on that ground.
2. "After Zee Telefilms, no writ lies against BCCI even in High Court." — WRONG. Right logic: Article 226 runs against "any person or authority", wider than Article 12; BCCI's public functions (selecting national team) attract 226.
3. "Article 32 is wider than Article 226 because it is a Fundamental Right." — WRONG on width. Right logic: Article 32 is stronger guarantee (itself a right) but narrower scope (needs State); Article 226 is wider scope (any person/authority).

## 17. Judges appointments — Three Judges Cases + NJAC (1981, 1993, 1998, 2015)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: consultation = concurrence after 1993; collegium (CJI + 4 seniors for SC) has primacy; NJAC (99th Amendment 2014) struck down 4:1 in Oct 2015 for breaking judicial independence (basic structure). Years: 1981 executive primacy, 1993 collegium born, 1998 procedure, 2015 NJAC gone. SI relevance: "Did NJAC replace collegium?" — No, struck down before working.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "First Judges Case (S.P. Gupta, 30 Dec 1981) held consultation means concurrence." — WRONG. Right logic: 1981 said consultation does NOT mean concurrence, executive had primacy; reversal came in Second Judges Case 1993.
2. "Collegium for SC appointments = CJI + 2 seniors / NJAC had no veto." — WRONG. Right logic: collegium = CJI + 4 seniors for SC (2 for HC, per 1998 reference); NJAC had CJI + 2 SC judges + Law Minister + 2 eminent persons, with any 2-member veto.
3. "99th Amendment / NJAC Act survived and collegium ended." — WRONG. Right logic: both struck down Oct 2015 (5-judge, 4:1) as violating independence of judiciary; collegium revived.

## 18. Birth of PIL (1979, 1981, 1984)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: locus relaxed for those who cannot come — letter can be writ; speedy trial + free legal aid read from Article 21 + 39A. Years: Hussainara Khatoon 1979 (first PIL, undertrials), S.P. Gupta 1981 (public-spirited person can move), Bandhua Mukti Morcha 1983/1984 (bonded labour). SI relevance: delay itself violates Article 21; legal aid is duty, not charity.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Only injured person can move court; letter cannot be petition." — WRONG after PIL. Right logic: S.P. Gupta 1981 allows bona fide public member for poor/disabled; epistolary jurisdiction treats letter as writ.
2. "Hussainara Khatoon held speedy trial is part of Article 21 and ordered releases." — RIGHT. Wrong version says it was about bonded labour — that is Bandhua Mukti Morcha (1984), stone quarries Haryana, Articles 21 + 23.
3. Assertion "Free legal aid is enforceable", Reason "Article 39A alone makes it enforceable." — A true, R true, but R does NOT explain A. Right logic: 39A is Directive; enforceable only when read INTO Article 21 fair procedure.

## 19. PUDR v. Union of India — Asiad workers (1982)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: pay below minimum wage = forced labour under Article 23; hunger/poverty is force, not only physical force; Article 23 binds private contractors, protects all persons. Year: 18 Sept 1982 (Asiad 1982, Rs 9.25/day). SI relevance: look for wages and debt, not chains — kiln paying below minimum on advance can attract Article 23 + Bonded Labour Act 1976.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Below-minimum wage is only Minimum Wages Act issue, not Article 23." — WRONG. Right logic: after PUDR 1982, economic compulsion = force; below-minimum = begar/other forced labour.
2. "Article 23 applies only against State / only to citizens." — WRONG. Right logic: Article 23 is horizontal, against private persons also, and protects all persons.
3. "Children on construction site is only Article 23." — WRONG. Right logic: children below 14 on hazardous construction also violates Article 24; punishment for Part III offences only by Parliament (Article 35) — hence central laws.

## 20. M.C. Mehta v. Tamil Nadu — Sivakasi children (1996)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: Article 24 bans only under-14 in factory/mine/hazardous work; Court ordered Rs 20,000 employer + Rs 5,000 govt per child into rehab fund + education + survey. Year: 10 Dec 1996. SI relevance: Articles 23-24 bind private employers directly; always check whether question asks Constitution (narrow) or 2016 Act (all work banned below 14).

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Article 24 bans all employment below 14." — WRONG for Constitution. Right logic: Article 24 bans only factory/mine/hazardous; ALL-employment ban below 14 comes from Child Labour Amendment Act 2016 (except family help after school + entertainment; adolescents 14-18 no hazardous; jail up to 2 yrs + Rs 50,000 fine).
2. "Sivakasi fund = Rs 20,000 employer + Rs 5,000 govt per child." — RIGHT. Wrong version swaps figures or says 25,000 employer — treat 20,000/5,000 as exam answer.
3. "Pulling child out is enough; no family support needed." — WRONG. Right logic: Court linked 24 with 21A/45 + 39(e)-(f); withdrawal + education + fund income, else family pushed deeper.

## 21. T.M.A. Pai — Who is minority? (2002)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: minority judged STATE-wise, not all-India; Article 30 needs established AND administered by minority; State can regulate for excellence but not destroy minority character. Year: 31 Oct 2002 (11 judges). SI relevance: unit question = State; Article 15(5) (93rd Amendment 2005) reservation in private unaided colleges expressly excludes Article 30 minority institutions.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Sikhs are minority everywhere because minority is all-India." — WRONG. Right logic: unit is State (States reorganised on language); Sikhs minority in India but majority in Punjab.
2. "Article 30 needs only administration, establishment not needed." — WRONG. Right logic: need BOTH limbs — established AND administered by minority.
3. "Government cannot fix teacher qualifications / ban profiteering in minority schools." — WRONG. Right logic: reasonable regulation for excellence allowed (qualified teachers, accounts, no capitation), but cannot take over so right becomes illusion; unaided autonomy subject to merit + transparency.

## 22. Sabarimala — PENDING, never settled (2018 onwards)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: 4:1 on 28 Sept 2018 struck bar on women 10-50, but 14 Nov 2019 (3:2) kept reviews pending and referred to larger bench; 9-judge started Jan-Feb 2020, revived April 2026 — NO final verdict yet. Years: 2018 + 2019 + 2020 + 2026 hearings. SI relevance: on duty/answer never quote 2018 as final; write "2018 (4:1) plus reference pending before nine judges".

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Sabarimala 2018 is settled law that bar is unconstitutional." — WRONG. Right logic: 2018 exists on paper, but larger reference unresolved and PENDING; correct exam answer is pending.
2. "Review petitions were rejected and no reference made." — WRONG. Right logic: 50+ reviews, 14 Nov 2019 kept pending + referred Articles 25/26, essential practices scope; 10 Feb 2020 reference held maintainable; notified 4 April 2026 bench under CJI Surya Kant hearing from 7 April 2026.
3. "Justice Indu Malhotra concurred with majority / devotees held separate denomination." — WRONG. Right logic: she dissented (courts should not lightly test deep faith); majority held devotees NOT separate denomination, bar NOT essential practice; Rule 3(b) Kerala 1965 Rules struck down on paper.

## 23. Maratha quota — 102nd and 105th (2021)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: Jaishri Patil 5 May 2021 struck Maratha quota (no SEBC proof, no extraordinary case to cross 50% Indra Sawhney) and 3:2 read 102nd (2018) as Centre-only SEBC list; 105th (19 Aug 2021) restored States' own lists. Years: 102nd 2018 (338B + 342A) — judgment May 2021 — 105th 2021. SI relevance: two amendments close together = judgment sits between.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Maratha quota upheld as SEBC with 64-65% total valid." — WRONG. Right logic: struck down — Marathas not shown SEBC, breach of 50% ceiling without extraordinary circumstances.
2. "102nd always allowed States to make SEBC lists." — WRONG as read in May 2021. Right logic: majority 3:2 (Rao, Gupta, Bhat; Bhushan + Nazeer dissenting on this point) read 342A as President + Parliament only; crisis froze State lists.
3. Assertion "Parliament passed 105th", Reason "102nd was read as taking away States' power." — Both TRUE and R EXPLAINS A. Right logic: 105th clarified Central list for Centre, State lists for States.

## 24. Janhit Abhiyan — EWS quota (2022)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: 103rd (2019) Articles 15(6)/16(6) — 10% EWS for non-SC/ST/OBC — upheld 3:2 on 7 Nov 2022; 50% ceiling NOT basic structure, EWS sits outside it. Year: 2022. SI relevance: unlearn "never cross 50%"; correct line: 50% governs Article 16(4) backward quotas, not EWS.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Any total above 50% is automatically unconstitutional after Indra Sawhney." — WRONG now. Right logic: Assertion false, Reason (Sawhney fixed ceiling) true; Janhit majority (Maheshwari, Trivedi, Pardiwala; dissent Lalit + Bhat) held ceiling is prudence for 16(4), not basic structure.
2. "EWS on economic criteria alone breaks basic structure / excluding SC/ST/OBC is discrimination." — WRONG per majority. Right logic: economic affirmative action allowed; exclusion avoids double benefit as they have own quotas (dissent disagreed, but majority is law).
3. "103rd cannot apply to private unaided institutions." — WRONG. Right logic: majority held Article 15(6) application to private unaided valid, without breaking basic structure.

## 25. Governor and Bills — EVOLVING, read as flux (2023, April 2025, Nov 2025)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: Article 200 silent on days → fight; all agree Governor cannot sit forever, but April 2025 vs Nov 2025 differ on timelines + deemed assent — NO final claim, position in flux. Years: Punjab Nov 2023 — TN 8 April 2025 — Reference 20 Nov 2025. SI relevance: university/police Bills affect your orders; courts can prod to decide, not sign as Governor.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Governor can sit indefinitely / pocket veto is allowed." — WRONG under both 2025 steps. Right logic: Punjab 2023 (Chandrachud) + both 2025 agree: cannot kill law-making by silence; withholding without return message violates Article 200.
2. "Tamil Nadu April 2025 (Pardiwala + Mahadevan, 10 Bills deemed assented + 1-3 month timelines) is final settled law." — WRONG, out of date if stated alone. Right logic: present BOTH — 20 Nov 2025 unanimous Reference (Gavai, on President's 14 questions of 13 May 2025) says NO judicial timelines, NO deemed assent via Article 142, elasticity preserved.
3. "After Nov 2025 courts cannot touch Governor inaction at all." — WRONG. Right logic: Nov 2025 allows limited mandamus to decide within reasonable time, without judging merits; Governor has choice (assent / withhold-cum-return / reserve; return barred for Money Bills), not strictly bound by Cabinet on choice, Article 361 immunity personal only.

## 26. I.R. Coelho — Ninth Schedule check (2007)

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: post-24 April 1973 Ninth Schedule insertions open to basic-structure challenge (effect/essence test on 14/19/21); pre-1973 stay sealed; Article 31B itself kept. Year: 11 Jan 2007 (9 judges, unanimous). SI relevance: "Ninth Schedule = full immunity" is false after 2007; cut-off date 24 April 1973 (Kesavananda date) is heart.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Any Ninth Schedule law is immune from Part III challenge." — WRONG. Right logic: Assertion false, Reason (Article 31B text protects) true; after Coelho 2007, post-1973 entries testable.
2. "Cut-off for Coelho review is 1973 / 1975 / First Amendment date." — Only 24 APRIL 1973 is RIGHT. Right logic: date of Kesavananda judgment; 1951 First Amendment created 31B/Schedule; Waman Rao 1981 background, reference from 1999.
3. "Coelho struck down Article 31B itself." — WRONG. Right logic: validity of 31B kept; only misuse after basic-structure date checked; amending power from Article 368 with limits; 284 entries by 2007, ~218 after 1973 showed misuse.

## 27. PESA 1996 — Separate panchayat law for Scheduled Areas

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: Part IX excluded by Article 243M from Fifth/Sixth Schedule + Nagaland/Meghalaya/Mizoram etc., restored with changes by PESA 24 Dec 1996 (Bhuria Committee) — Gram Sabha is boss. Year: 1996. SI relevance (AP — ASR, Parvathipuram Manyam, Eluru, Palnadu, Srikakulam, Anakapalli, Kakinada): check Gram Sabha resolution before obstruction case on minor minerals/land.

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Part IX directly applies to Scheduled Areas / PESA is 73rd Amendment itself." — WRONG. Right logic: excluded by 243M (protective, to save traditional self-rule); extended by Parliament under 243M(4)(b) via PESA.
2. "Sarpanch/Panchayat is supreme under PESA; Gram Sabha only advisory." — WRONG. Right logic: Gram Sabha (all voters) supreme — approves plans/beneficiaries, consulted on land acquisition/rehabilitation, restores tribal land, owns minor forest produce.
3. "PESA Gram Sabha has no say on intoxicants/money-lending/minor minerals." — WRONG. Right logic: five-word key — land, minor forest produce, minor minerals (recommendation for lease), intoxicants (regulate/stop), money-lending + minor water bodies/markets.

## 28. Andhra chain — Sri Bagh to 371D

**TAKEAWAY (5 lines max):**
Principle: each pact calms smaller region's fear (jobs/seats/capital) before merger: Sri Bagh 1937 (Coastal-Rayalaseema) → Gentlemen's 1956 → Six Points 1973 → 32nd Amendment + 371D/371E. Years: 16 Nov 1937, 20 Feb 1956, 1 Nov 1956 AP, 21 Sept 1973. SI relevance: zone-wise SI posting = 371D Presidential Order 1975 (local cadres, local candidates).

**HOW ASKED:**
1. "Gentlemen's Agreement came after AP formation to solve disputes." — WRONG. Right logic: 20 Feb 1956 BEFORE 1 Nov 1956; promise (Regional Council, jobs/education safeguards, Deputy CM convention) led to formation.
2. "Six-Point Formula came after 32nd Amendment / Mulki Rules continue." — WRONG. Right logic: Formula 21 Sept 1973 → 32nd Amendment (1973, passed 1974) → 371D (President by ORDER, local cadres) + 371E (Central University Hyderabad 1974); Mulki abolished. Sri Bagh house = Kashinathuni Nageswara Rao's Sri Bagh, Madras.
3. "Article 16(3) = 371D / 371D ended in 2014 bifurcation." — WRONG. Right logic: 16(3) Parliament by LAW residence rule; 371D President by ORDER local cadres in AP; 371D survives for both AP + Telangana after 2014.

## 29. Potti Sriramulu — Two birthdays 1953 and 1956

Years: 1 Oct 1953 (Kurnool, Prakasam) vs 1 Nov 1956 (Hyderabad, Sanjiva Reddy). SI relevance: never write "1953 on SRC recommendation" — SRC reported 1955, after 1953.

**HOW ASKED:**

2. "1956 Andhra Pradesh = Andhra State + Telugu Hyderabad areas by Reorganisation Act + 7th Amendment." — RIGHT. Wrong version says capital Kurnool / CM Prakasam for 1956 — those belong to 1953; 1956 capital Hyderabad, CM Neelam Sanjiva Reddy.
3. "Bapatla 1913 = Hyderabad Andhra Maha Sabha / Dar + JVP favoured immediate linguistic states." — WRONG. Right logic: Bapatla May 1913 (B.N. Sarma) is Madras Telugu demand; Hyderabad Sabha first at Jogipet 1930 different; Dar (10 Dec 1948) + JVP (1 April 1949) said NO to pure language, JVP allowed Andhra only if Madras city claim dropped.
