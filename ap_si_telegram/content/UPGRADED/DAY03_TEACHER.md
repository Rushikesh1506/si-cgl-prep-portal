My dear aspirant, yesterday rights you can take to court. Today duties of state you cannot directly demand, but which guide every law and your diary. Think of them as moral compass.
Picture a Kurnool village. Landless labour, kids out of school, women no maternity help, court far, liquor near school. The Constitution tells the state to secure welfare, cut income gaps, equal pay, protect children, free legal aid, work and maternity relief, strong panchayats, SC ST care, nutrition health, ban harmful drinks except medicine, protect cattle farming, early care till six, forests wildlife, monuments, separate judiciary, world peace. These are Directive Principles for Centre State local bodies when making laws.
Remember three baskets. Socialist for equality welfare, Gandhian for villages weaker sections, liberal for modern ideas like common civil code environment judiciary. Understand heart not numbers. Equal justice legal aid, living wage, worker share, maternity, health, panchayats, SC ST, environment, heritage, separation.
Important trap. If law breaks rights court can strike. But narrow shield: laws for sharing resources and stopping wealth concentration cannot be struck merely for breaking equality or speech-movement freedoms. Parliament once stretched shield to all welfare goals, Minerva Mills cancelled extension. Shield stays narrow to those two resource goals. Nine judges in 2024 said not all private property is community resource, do not stretch. You do not decide but know why land reforms survive.
Duties of citizens are ten plus one. Ten in 1976, one more in 2002 for parents to school kids six-fourteen. Respect Constitution flag anthem, freedom ideals, unity integrity, defend country, harmony beyond religion language, culture, environment animals, science temper, public property non-violence, excellence, child schooling. Not directly jailable, but laws can enforce like flag environment schooling. When youth burn buses counsel in this language.
As police note equal justice free legal aid. State must not let poverty deny justice. Tell poor arrestee about aid, produce before court. New BNSS stresses lawyer-aid for poor. If daily wager cannot hire, help get aid. Denial hurts person and case.
Worker child health liquor raids are constitutional work. Raiding child labour, no maternity unit, illicit liquor near school fulfils directions. Write diary in that spirit.
Rights vs directions clash: housing law takes plot, owner cries equality, state cries welfare. Court balances, rights generally prevail, harmony tried. Minerva said balance is basic structure.
Chart: three baskets, narrow shield 2024 reaffirmed, ten plus one duties, legal aid in every arrest. When tribal woman in Alluri asks who fights for me, answer aid not silence.
