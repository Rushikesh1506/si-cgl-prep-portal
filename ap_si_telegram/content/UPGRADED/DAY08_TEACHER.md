My dear future SI, imagine night duty in Guntur. You hold a boy for questioning, his poor mother waits outside with no lawyer. That one scene holds the full judiciary chapter.

Our top court in Delhi is small, the Chief Justice plus 33 judges, total 34. Judges are picked by collegium, the Chief Justice with four senior judges. Parliament once made a new commission law to change this, but the court struck it down in 2015. That was NJAC. Collegium survives. As SI you must see why. A fearless judge alone can free an innocent person.

For that mother there are writ roads. The top court's writ power is called heart and soul, but it is narrow, only for fundamental rights. The High Court at Amaravati is wider, for fundamental rights plus other legal rights. Never swap this. If remand is illegal or a legal right is denied, Amaravati can help even when Delhi writ route cannot. In station work, illegal detention invites habeas corpus, produce the body. Mandamus compels duty, prohibition stops excess, quo warranto questions false office claim, certiorari corrects grave error.

Other powers matter. Centre-State fights start directly in top court, that is original jurisdiction. If President is doubtful on law, he can seek advice, that advice guides but does not bind like a judgment. A judge can be removed only for proved misbehaviour or incapacity by special majority in both Houses. You can be suspended by an officer, a judge cannot. That safety guards independence.

Then the auditor, CAG. He audits all Centre and State money, holds six years or till 65 whichever earlier, removed like a top judge, reports examined by Public Accounts Committee. Your malkhana register is the same habit on small scale.

PIL is the human heart. In Hussainara Khatoon and Khatri cases for Bihar undertrials, then S.P. Gupta case, court said any good person can file for the poor who cannot come. Even a postcard can start a case, called epistolary jurisdiction. Locus is diluted for the voiceless, never for private grudge or publicity.

Under new BNSS and BNS, produce arrested person before magistrate within 24 hours, give reasons for remand, respect bail. If you fail, habeas will test you.

Last fine point. Court as judge is not State, court as office is State. Tender, recruitment by registrar can face equality test, but judgment itself cannot be called discrimination. This is Mirajkar and Antulay rule. Remember, your lathi is strong because a judge is independent, accounts are audited, and a poor letter can reach Delhi.
