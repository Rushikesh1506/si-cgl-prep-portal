# DAY 06 TEACHER - President/Governor/VP
Think of President as SHO on paper: all files in his name, real probe by Council of Ministers. Without signature nothing moves.

**Election:** Indirect. Elected MPs of both Houses + elected MLAs of States plus Delhi/Puducherry vote. Nominated MPs, nominated MLAs, all MLCs do NOT vote. Weight by population. Term 5 yrs, re-eligible. Impeachment for violation of Constitution: 1/4th notice, 2/3rd majority in House.

**Assent and veto:** Bill becomes law only after assent. Choices: assent, withhold, return once for reconsideration. If House passes again, must assent. Suspensive veto only once. On Money Bill no return. Pocket veto by indefinite delay possible in practice.

**Ordinance 123:** When Parliament not in session and immediate action needed, President legislates. Temporary: must be laid before House, dies automatically 6 weeks after reassembly if not passed. D.C. Wadhwa condemned Bihar repromulgation without House as fraud.

**Pardon 72:** Pardon, commute, remit, reprieve, respite in death, court-martial, Union law. Not arbitrary. Epuru Sudhakar from AP holds limited judicial review for mala fide.

**VP:** Elected by all MPs elected+nominated, no MLAs. Ex-officio RS Chairman. Acts as President max 6 months. Removal by RS majority plus LS concurrence with 14 days notice.

**Governor:** Appointed by President, holds during pleasure. Discretion in hung House, 356 report, reserving bills. On bills 200: assent, withhold, return once except Money Bill, reserve for President. Ordinance 213 mirrors Union six-week rule.

**91st Amendment 2003:** Ministers max 15% of House strength, min 12. Defector disqualified under 10th Schedule barred as minister till re-elected. No jumbo cabinets.
Line: elected-only elect President, veto once, ordinance 6 weeks, pardon reviewable, Governor mirrors with 200/213.
