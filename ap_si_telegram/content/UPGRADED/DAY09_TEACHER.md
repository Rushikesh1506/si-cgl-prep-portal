My dear future officer, come with me to a village near Vizianagaram. The sarpanch is upset, road money has not come, the Mandal officer says wait for Finance Commission. Villagers ask who owns our water and lights. That is local bodies.

First our villages only had a hope in the Constitution, then in early nineties they got real teeth. Villages got Part Nine with 29 subjects like agriculture, drinking water, minor irrigation, primary school, health. Towns got Part Nine-A with 18 functions like urban plan, roads, sanitation, slums. Hold like two hands, villages 29, towns 18. Exam loves to swap them, never let it.

Villages have three floors. Bottom Gram Panchayat, middle Mandal or Block, top Zilla Parishad. Tiny States below twenty lakh people may skip middle floor. Towns have Nagar Panchayat for rising town, Council for small town, Corporation for big city like Vizag and Vijayawada.

Seats come by direct election. SC ST seats in proportion to population, women one-third by Constitution. AP by own law gives half seats to women. Term five years, if dissolved early fresh polls within six months. You will guard these polls, not conduct them. State Election Commission conducts local polls. Money share is advised by State Finance Commission every five years.

Some hilly north-east council areas are kept outside this system to guard customs. That exclusion is important. For plans, District Planning Committee joins village and town plans, Metro Committee plans big cities.

AP follows AP Panchayat Raj Act 1994. After 26 districts in 2022, maps changed but tiers stayed. In agency hills of Alluri Sitharama Raju and Parvathipuram Manyam, the 1996 PESA law rules. There Gram Sabha of all voters is supreme. No land taking, no mineral lease, no liquor shop without its consent. As SI there, protect tribal land, stop illegal grabbing, respect Sabha, enforce law with consent. When sarpanch asks for funds, tell him funds follow Finance advice, audit watches, SEC holds polls, DPC plans. Your duty is peaceful poll and fair dispute guard. You are the friend of grassroots democracy.
