My dear future SI, picture a festival evening in an AP town. A boy shouts against the MLA on a mike past midnight. A group wants to march with sticks. An auto driver says I will live and vend anywhere. All shout freedom. You must not laugh nor lathi-charge at once. You must know what freedom is and where it stops.
Our Constitution gives citizens six freedoms. To speak and express, to assemble peacefully without arms, to form associations, to move freely, to reside anywhere, to do any profession or business. Only citizens get them. The old property freedom was removed, do not search for it.
Limits are your real work. Speech can be limited for sovereignty and integrity, security, foreign friendship, public order, decency-morality, contempt, defamation, incitement to crime. Assembly and association for sovereignty-integrity, public order, morality. Movement-residence for public interest and tribal protection. Profession for qualifications and state-run services. Only a fair written law can limit, not personal order.
If a youth falsely posts the MLA takes bribes, that can be defamation. The new BNS keeps defamation punishable. This is a valid speech limit. But saying roads are bad is criticism you must protect. Your FIR must show this difference.
Three shields protect every person in crime cases. No punishment for an act not a crime on that date, no heavier penalty than that date. No second prosecution and punishment for same offence. No force to speak against self. No beating for confession. These cover citizen and foreigner.
During 1975 Emergency many were jailed without remedy and ADM Jabalpur said no help. The nation called it wrong. So the 44th Amendment wrote that criminal shields and life-liberty can never be suspended even in Emergency. It reversed that mistake.
Life-liberty is your guide. Maneka Gandhi lost her passport without reason and fought. The Court said procedure must be fair just reasonable, not any typed rule. So life grew to dignity, food, health, speedy trial, privacy in Puttaswamy. Before checking a phone or detaining, ask is my power legal and fair.
Every child six to fourteen must get free compulsory schooling by the 86th Amendment and RTE law. A hotel using an eight-year-old must be acted on with educators.
On arrest tell grounds in simple words, allow lawyer and family call, produce before magistrate within twenty-four hours excluding travel. BNSS repeats this. For women avoid night arrest after sunset before sunrise unless urgent with prior magistrate permission. Memo diary register will save you.
Preventive detention is not punishment but prevention of future danger. Beyond three months needs Advisory Board opinion. Tell grounds quickly, allow representation and forward fast. Delay alone can invalidate. You rarely order it but you serve papers. Protect speech, stop incitement, allow unarmed assembly, never punish retroactively, never force confession, keep procedure fair, school children, produce in time.
