# DAY 05 TEACHER - Emergency: 352/356/360 + 33-34
Dear aspirant, imagine you are SHO when radio says war at border. What can you do? What can you NEVER do? That line is Emergency law.

**1. National Emergency - ground changed:** 1975 Emergency on vague internal disturbance led to arrests, censorship. 44th Amendment deleted internal disturbance, put armed rebellion. Now only 3 grounds: war, external aggression, armed rebellion. A city riot or student agitation is law-order under BNSS, not national emergency.

**2. Check and balance:** President proclaims only on written advice of Union Cabinet, not PM alone orally. Parliament approval in 1 month by special majority, renewal every 6 months. Lok Sabha can revoke: 1/10th members notice forces special sitting in 14 days, disapproval by simple majority compels President to revoke.

**3. Rights effect - SI must memorise:** If ground is war/external aggression, freedoms under Art 19 auto-suspend. If ground is armed rebellion, no auto-suspension. Separate Presidential order needed for other rights. But Arts 20-21 never suspend. No retrospective punishment, no double jeopardy, no forced confession, no torture, production before magistrate continues. Custodial violence remains crime even in war.

**4. State Emergency 356:** President acts on Governor report or otherwise satisfied from other inputs. Duration: 2 months without Parliament, then up to 1 yr, max 3 yrs with conditions. Bommai 1994 chained misuse: secularism basic, floor test mandatory, courts can review mala fide, assembly can be revived. Sarkaria said use as last resort after warning. AP saw 356 phases; during 356 Governor acts for President but your BNSS duties continue.

**5. Financial Emergency 360 - never used:** On threat to financial stability/credit. Directions on pay, money bills reserve. Never proclaimed till date. 1991 crisis did not use it.

**6. Arts 33-34:** Art 33 lets Parliament curb rights of forces/police for discipline, so conduct rules bar politics. Art 34 lets Parliament indemnify bona fide acts under martial law, which is military rule in area, different from emergency.
Remember: 352 nation danger, LS kill-switch, 20-21 immortal. 356 state failure, floor test, 3-yr cap. 360 money danger, never used.
