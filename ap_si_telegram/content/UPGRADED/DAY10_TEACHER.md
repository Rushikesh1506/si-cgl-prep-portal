My dear aspirant, amendments are like repairs to a house. Our house started in 1950, then family grew, courts spoke, governments learned. Each big repair has a number and a story, and as SI you must not mix them.

First the 42nd repair in Emergency time, called mini Constitution. It tried to make directive principles stronger than rights and gave wide shield to laws. Court later cut that wide shield. Then 44th repair undid Emergency excess, removed property as fundamental right, made it legal right, and guarded liberty and Emergency misuse.

52nd gave anti-defection law, if you cross floor for money or post you lose seat, decided by Speaker with court review. 61st made vote age 18, youth power. 73rd and 74th gave village and town self-rule you already studied, 29 and 18 lists, women third, AP half. 86th made schooling for 6 to 14 a fundamental right and duty.

91st capped ministers at fifteen percent of House strength and tightened defection, no split escape. Never swap 91st with quota. 93rd allowed quotas in private colleges except minority institutions. 97th gave cooperatives a place, right to form them. 101st brought GST with council where Centre States decide tax together.

103rd gave ten percent EWS quota among forward poor. Remember EWS only via 103rd, never via 91st. 104th ended Anglo-Indian nominated seats and extended SC ST reservation in legislatures.

Two current anchors. In Nov 2022 court upheld EWS, that is Janhit Abhiyan. In 2024 nine judges explained when private wealth can be community resource. Women quota law of 2023 waits for census and delimitation, not yet working. As SI, you stop booth capturing for youth vote, guard school attendance, enforce defection peace outside House, respect EWS certificate checks without bias. House repairs protect your beat.
