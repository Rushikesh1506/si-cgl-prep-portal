My dear aspirant, today we open the Constitution itself and ask who belongs, who is state, and what is law. Imagine you are an SI verifying a candidate, a tenant, a protest pamphlet.
First the promise at the gate. The Preamble speaks justice equality liberty fraternity, unity integrity dignity. Three words Socialist Secular Integrity came by the 42nd Amendment in 1976. Socialist means welfare gap reduction not state ownership of all. Secular means no state religion equal respect. Integrity added with unity to stress one nation. In Berubari the Court once said Preamble not part, but in Kesavananda it held Preamble is part and can be amended like other parts, yet basic structure cannot be destroyed. So Parliament can add words but cannot kill soul.
Next who is citizen. From 1950 Articles five to eleven fixed starting citizenship. Those domiciled with birth or parent link or five-year stay became citizens at start. Those who came from Pakistan with permits and stay steps got citizenship with cut dates. Those who left to Pakistan after March 1947 lost claim. Indians abroad with parent-grandparent link could register. One who voluntarily takes foreign citizenship loses Indian tag. Continuance and Parliament power to make law cover rest. Parliament made the 1955 Act for birth descent registration naturalisation. As SI you check Aadhaar voter not proof alone, you forward to Foreigners officer, do not declare citizenship yourself.
Who is state for rights cases. Government and Parliament of India, governments and legislatures of states, local bodies like panchayat municipality, and other authorities under control. Courts read other authorities to include bodies with state-like power and funding control per Rajasthan Board Sukhdev Ajay Hasia tests like LIC ONGC. A purely private shop is not state, but a private school doing public duty with aid may face scrutiny. When a constable beats, when a municipality demolishes, state action can be challenged.
What is law that cannot break rights. Pre-British and British laws in force, plus new Acts ordinances orders rules notifications that act like law. Custom with force of law counts. But constitutional amendment is not ordinary law for this test. Story: Golaknath said Parliament cannot amend rights, Parliament replied by 24th Amendment writing that amendment is not law under this bar. Kesavananda upheld that power but said basic structure stays. So amendment can change but not destroy.
For SI work: respect Preamble values in beat, verify citizenship via records not bias especially near borders, know state includes your station, and know any order you enforce must respect rights unless valid amendment changed them.
