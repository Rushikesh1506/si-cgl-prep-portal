1. Champakam Dorairajan (1951)
TAKEAWAY:
Fundamental Rights beat Directive Principles — Art 29(2) killed the Communal G.O. that denied admission only on caste.
Year: 1951. Bench: early SC; led to First Amendment inserting Art 15(4).
SI cares: you enforce rights on the ground; no government order or goal can justify caste/religion-based denial of a right.
HOW ASKED:
a) Match trap — "Champakam — Art 15(4) origin / First Amendment 1951." Right: 15(4) came AFTER as reply; Wrong: 15(4) existed before or case created reservation directly.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: FR prevails over DPSP in 1951. S2: Art 37 says DPSP not enforceable in court. S3: Order void under Art 13. All three true; trap option drops S2 to make you doubt.
c) Scenario — Brahmin girl denied medical seat despite higher marks due to community quota. Question: violation? Right: Art 29(2); Wrong: Art 16 (jobs) or Art 46 (DPSP defence — State actually argued this and lost).

2. E.P. Royappa (1973 decided / quoted 1974)
TAKEAWAY:
Equality = no arbitrariness. Art 14 broken even with no group classification if State act has no reason.
Year/Bench: 1974, 5 judges, Bhagwati opinion — "equality and arbitrariness are sworn enemies."
SI cares: any station order, transfer, seizure, or lock-up decision without recorded reason can fail Art 14.
HOW ASKED:
a) Quote match — "Equality and arbitrariness are sworn enemies / cribbed, cabined and confined." Right: Royappa; Wrong: Maneka (fair procedure) or Sawhney (reservation).
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: Royappa lost on facts (same pay, no bad faith proved). S2: Idea won — new arbitrariness door added to Art 14. Trap says "Royappa won his post back" — false.
c) Scenario — Officer transferred to same-pay side post with no file reason, claims Art 14. Right: can plead arbitrariness even with no group comparison; Wrong: "Art 14 needs two groups, so no case" — that is pre-Royappa law.

3. Maneka Gandhi (1978)
TAKEAWAY:
Art 21 procedure must be right, just and fair, not any paper procedure. Gopalan (1950) narrow view overruled.
Arts 14-19-21 linked as Golden Triangle; hearing + reasons read into liberty.
SI cares: arrest/detention/search lawful only if section + fair method — inform grounds, hear, record reasons. Root of DK Basu.
HOW ASKED:
a) Chain match — Gopalan 1950 narrow / Maneka 1978 fair / Puttaswamy 2017 privacy. Trap jumbles years or says Maneka upheld Gopalan.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: Passport impounded without reasons/hearing. S2: Court demanded fair procedure. S3: Arts 14,19,21 separate rooms. S3 false — they work together; option "all true" is trap.
c) Scenario — Warden/officer says "rule book allows, so no need to give reason." Right: violates Maneka; Wrong: valid because "procedure established by law exists" — dead Gopalan logic.

4. Indra Sawhney / Mandal (1992)
TAKEAWAY:
27% OBC quota upheld; Art 16(4) is not exception but emphatic statement of Art 16(1) equality; 50% ceiling (normal), creamy layer out, no promotion quota then, no 10% poor-only quota.
Year/Bench: 1992, 9 judges, 6:3. Later: 77th/81st/85th Amendments + Janhit Abhiyan 2022 EWS update.
SI cares: roster, reservation, creamy-layer exclusion in recruitment you will face and implement without bias.
HOW ASKED:
a) Bench/year match — "9 judges, 1992, 50% ceiling + creamy layer." Wrong options: 1973 / 5 judges (Royappa/Kesavananda mix) or "Mandal gave EWS quota" — EWS is 103rd Amendment + 2022.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: 16(4) is exception to 16(1). False — it is emphatic restatement. S2: Creamy layer must be excluded. True. S3: Reservation can never cross 50%. False now — extraordinary cases + EWS outside. "All true" is trap.
c) Scenario — Dept reserves 65% + gives promotion quota under unamended 16(4). Right: violates Sawhney as decided in 1992 (needs 77th Amendment/extraordinary reason); Wrong: fully valid under Mandal.

5. Kesavananda Bharati (1973)
TAKEAWAY:

Year/Bench: 24 April 1973, 13 judges, 7:6; Preamble is part of Constitution; no closed list of basic features.
SI cares: Constitution supremacy, rule of law, judicial review protect your uniform authority — no order above basic structure stands.
HOW ASKED:
a) Numbers match — "13 judges, 7:6, 1973, basic structure born." Wrong: 9 judges (Sawhney/Bommai) or 6:5 (Golaknath) or "full final list given" — court deliberately left open.
b) Chain trap — Shankari Prasad 1951 yes / Sajjan Singh 1965 yes / Golaknath 1967 no / 24th Amendment 1971 yes / Kesavananda middle path. Trap says Golaknath still holds.
c) Scenario — Amendment removes judicial review and says unchallengeable. Right: void — violates basic structure; Wrong: valid because "Parliament amending power unlimited."

6. Minerva Mills (1980)
TAKEAWAY:
Struck 42nd Amendment clauses trying to make amending power unlimited and shielding ANY DPSP law from Arts 14/19. 31C back to only 39(b)-(c). FR-DPSP balance itself is basic structure.
Year: 1980; follow-up: Property Owners Assn (Nov 2024, 9 judges) narrowed 39(b).
SI cares: neither rights nor goals crush each other — you balance duty (DPSP aim) with rights procedure daily.
HOW ASKED:
a) Match trap — "Minerva 1980 — cut back 42nd Amendment, 31C = only 39(b)+(c)." Wrong: "any DPSP law safe from 14/19" — that is dead 42nd version.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: Limited power cannot make itself unlimited. True. S2: Judicial review is basic structure. True. S3: Balance of FR-DPSP is basic structure. True. Trap adds S4: "All private property is 39(b) material" — rejected in 2024.
c) Scenario — Law for Art 47 (health) claims immunity from Art 14 challenge citing 31C. Right: no shield — only true 39(b)/(c) laws; Wrong: shielded because "any DPSP."

7. Balaji Raghavan (decided Dec 1995 / quoted 1996)
TAKEAWAY:
Bharat Ratna/Padma awards (from Jan 1954 notifications) are NOT titles under Art 18(1) — valid, but no prefix/suffix use; misuse can lose award under Regulation 10.
SI cares: protocol at functions — never write "Padma Shri X" as rank; correct honour without creating class.
HOW ASKED:
a) Direct match — "National awards are not titles — Balaji Raghavan 1996." Wrong: "awards banned as titles" or year 1992/1994 mix.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: 18(1) bars State titles except military/academic. True. S2: Awards cannot be prefix/suffix. True. S3: Citizen can take foreign title with President consent. False — 18(2) full bar; consent is only 18(3) for non-citizen on office of profit.
c) Scenario — Invitation prints "Padma Bhushan Ramesh Kumar IPS (Retd)". Right: misuse — recognition allowed, rank-use barred; Wrong: cancel award automatically or "prefix allowed."

8. Kathi Kalu Oghad (1961) + Selvi (2010)
TAKEAWAY:
Art 20(3) bars compelled testimony (mind), not body material. Fingerprints, handwriting specimen, blood/DNA, photos, ID parade allowed; forced confession / mind-revealing paper barred. Selvi: forced narco/polygraph/BEAP violates 20(3)+21.
Year/Bench: Kathi Kalu 1961, 11 judges; Selvi 2010; needs accused + compulsion; covers investigation stage.
SI cares: you CAN take prints/specimen/DNA; you CANNOT force confession or narco without free magistrate-recorded consent + lawyer safeguards.
HOW ASKED:
a) Classic false statement — "Forcing specimen handwriting violates 20(3)." False — allowed. Right: forced confession / forced narco violates. Trap flips them.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: To be witness = share personal knowledge. True. S2: Body measurements are testimony. False. S3: Selvi result alone is not confession; only later-found material via S.27 Evidence Act usable. True. "All true" is trap.
c) Scenario — Accused refuses fingerprint vs refuses narco without consent. Right: compel first, not second; Wrong: both barred or both compellable.

9. ADM Jabalpur (1976)

HOW ASKED:
a) Chain match — "ADM Jabalpur 1976, 4:1, Khanna dissent — 44th Amendment 1978 non-suspendable 20/21 — Puttaswamy 2017 overruled." Wrong: "2:1" or "21 still suspendable."
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: Internal disturbance was Emergency ground. True then. S2: Now replaced by armed rebellion. True. S3: Art 19 auto-shut under 358 still same. False — limited to war/external aggression now.
c) Scenario — Detainee under MISA 1975 asks court to check legality. Right per majority then: no standing; per law today: maintainable — trap asks "apply 1976 rule today" — wrong.

10. Puttaswamy (2017)
TAKEAWAY:
Privacy IS Fundamental Right under Art 21 + Part III; M.P. Sharma 1954 + Kharak Singh 1962 overruled on this; also overruled ADM Jabalpur. Limit needs legality + legitimate aim + proportionality.
Year/Bench: 24 Aug 2017, 9 judges, unanimous. Aadhaar background.
SI cares: tapping, phone/laptop search, body data, CCTV retention need law + narrow need; overreach = Art 21 violation.
HOW ASKED:
a) Direct match — "Privacy FR — Puttaswamy 2017." Wrong: 1978 (Maneka) or "absolute right" — it is not absolute.
b) Three-test trap — Legality + legitimate aim + proportionality all needed. Option dropping proportionality or adding "public sentiment" is wrong.
c) Scenario — Officer opens suspect phone "for quick check, no law cited." Right: violates Puttaswamy; Wrong: allowed because "privacy not FR" — dead Sharma/Kharak view.

14. Epuru Sudhakar (2006, Andhra Pradesh)
TAKEAWAY:
Pardon (Art 72 President / Art 161 Governor) reviewable on limited grounds — no application of mind, mala fide, irrelevant/extraneous ground, hidden/ignored facts. 72 covers court martial + can pardon death; 161 covers neither (only suspend/remit/commute death in State-law setting); on ministerial advice.
SI cares: AP case — expect in State paper; remission order built on politics/false record can be quashed and sent for fresh consideration.
HOW ASKED:
a) 72 vs 161 match — "72: court martial yes + death pardon yes; 161: both no." Trap flips or says Governor pardons death fully.
b) Multi-statement trap — S1: Clemency fully beyond courts. False after 2006. S2: Review on mala fide/irrelevant/no mind/wrong facts. True. S3: Governor acts on personal choice. False — on Council advice.
c) Scenario — Governor remits political-killer sentence citing "good political worker" + wrong facts. Right: set aside under Epuru Sudhakar, liberty to reconsider; Wrong: immune mercy.
